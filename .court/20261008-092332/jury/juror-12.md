VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: As described, the applicant signs public supply contracts as the sole contractual party, with no stated legal form, no secured supplier terms and no checked suitability, so every non-delivery or defect lands on them personally, and one badly performed contract can count against them in later tenders.
DECIDING FACT: The prosecution's point, which the defense concedes ("Die Haftung liegt tatsächlich beim Antragsteller"), that the authority contracts with the applicant and not the wholesaler, combined with "Rechtsform" being listed as unknown and the cited risk that significant poor performance is an optional exclusion ground under § 124 GWB.
WOULD FLIP IF: The case stated a liability-limiting legal form plus written wholesaler commitments (delivery on credit, delivery deadlines and warranty passed back to the wholesaler) for a defined product category, and started with small contracts whose suitability requirements the applicant can verifiably meet.
