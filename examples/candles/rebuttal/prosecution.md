# Prosecution rebuttal

1. **Survives in part.** Someone paid, but the plan needs volume, the very thing the defense sets aside, and who bought and whether sales are rising, which the defense says "decides if demand is real," is unrecorded.

2. **Survives as arithmetic only.** Five months counts rent alone, while sales are gross, with candle cost, fees and other living costs unrecorded. Covering rent from gross sales needs about 57 sales a month, over four times today's roughly 13.

3. **Does not survive.** "Spare time" shows hours are scarce, not that hours limit sales, and the defense admits "the record does not say which." If demand is the cap, extra hours add stock, not sales, and the record does not say whether a cheaper test exists.

4. **Survives, but beside the point.** No lease is added, yet quitting ends the nursing income, whose amount the record never states, and the defense's own runway has savings paying the rent while the business covers under a quarter of it. Whether home production can scale is, by the defense's own list, unknown.

**Decide point 3.** Points 1, 2 and 4 describe where the user starts and how long the savings last. Point 3 decides whether the plan can pay off, and the defense concedes the record is silent.
