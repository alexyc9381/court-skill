VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: I wouldn't let anyone I love quit nursing and stake the $8,000 they have saved on candles that gross about $373 a month against $1,600 rent, with no stop point and no word on whether they could go back.
DECIDING FACT: 40 sales at $28 over three months is about $373 a month, which the defense concedes covers under a quarter of the $1,600 rent, so at today's pace the $8,000 saved is gone in under seven months even if every dollar of sales were profit and rent were the only bill.
WOULD FLIP IF: the record showed a firm way back to nursing work, such as an approved leave or a promised re-hire, so that failing would cost the savings but not the nursing income for good.
