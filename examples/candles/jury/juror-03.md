VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: As the lawyer, I see a plan that gives up the paycheck before papering anything: candles made at home carry fire, burn and lease exposure, and the record shows no liability insurance, lease permission, tax plan, health coverage or license upkeep behind them, only an $8,000 cushion that is five months of rent.
DECIDING FACT: "They make the candles at home" and rent is $1,600 a month, so a wax-and-flame business sits in what is presumably a leased home, yet the record is silent on the lease, any insurance and which country's rules apply.
WOULD FLIP IF: The case showed the home business already papered: the landlord's written consent to run it from the home and an insurance policy covering product liability for it.
